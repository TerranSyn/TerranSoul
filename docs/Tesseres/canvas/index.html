Problem

1. AI is replacing people — and the skilled first: programmers, lawyers, accountants, designers, data analysts. 2. Governments promise AI will create more jobs than it destroys, but nobody has explained where those jobs come from. 3. The deeper risk is concentration of power: a few companies own the core AI technology, and everyone else has to rent intelligence instead of owning it.

Existing Alternatives

Today the choice is: automate and cut jobs, or rent intelligence from a handful of foundation-model providers — paying monthly for a system you cannot control, cannot customize, and do not own.

Solution

Reinstate work instead of replacing it — the mission's three ways: 1. TerranSoul lets anyone start and run a business just by talking with AI. 2. Tessera always requires a human to approve and take responsibility for every AI action. 3. Businesses own their AI capability (memory + execution) instead of renting it.

Key Metrics

New businesses started via TerranSoul (new economic activity created, not jobs replaced).

Unique Value Proposition

Sovereign AI — owned by people, not rented from Big Tech.

High-Level Concept

When code becomes nearly free, own what stays.

Unfair Advantage

Published, benchmarked AI-memory research (TerranSoul: 99.4% LongMemEval-S R@5, 9.82/10 personal-AI answer quality at 1.06s latency — measured, not claimed) plus a working AI-driven integration engine (Tessera: 66 Enterprise Integration Patterns implemented, 3,800+ tests, 8 interchangeable brokers) that a fundraised competitor (Matillion MAIA) is only now building toward.

Customer Segments

The mission's three: (1) Individuals — knowledge workers whose work AI has displaced, who want to start something new. (2) Businesses that want to own their AI capability instead of renting it. (3) Nations that want sovereign AI infrastructure.

Early Adopters

Freecracy — HR AI-agent platform (“Talents Force”).

Cost Structure

Founder & engineering time (2 co-founders); cloud + LLM API costs, which fall over time as TerranSoul shifts solved work onto the frozen local model instead of paid inference.

Revenue Streams

Primary: equity ownership in partner businesses — we take a % stake and grow as our partners grow. This is the largest upside, and it only pays off if the partner's business actually succeeds.
